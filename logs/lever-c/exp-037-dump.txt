Notes
-----

* The four candidates are the intersection of `/sys/bus/spmi/devices/`
  (`0-00 0-01 0-05 0-07`) with the debugfs regmap directories. `0-07` is
  spmi address 0x07 = pm8550b, the repeater's parent PMIC.
* `0-00`, `0-01`, `0-05` print 92 lines of `XX`: their `max_register` is
  below 0xfd00, so `regmap_readable()` is false and debugfs prints `XX`
  without a hardware read.
* The `RPT-SCAN` line for `0-07` carries the signature
  (`08=80 46=80`) that selected it; the full block is `RPT-B`.
* `timeouts before=0` / `after=0`: no `pmic_arb_wait_for_done` timeouts —
  the "676 unimplemented registers × 1 ms" model of §12.2 does not happen
  in practice (fast arbiter error).
